The programme

Three months to feel like yourself again, and stay that way.

The you who wakes up with energy, moves without overthinking it, and eats well without obsessing. This programme makes her your everyday, not just your good weeks.

What's included

4 private online sessions a month

50 minutes each, mixing yoga, Pilates and mobility depending on what you need. Some weeks that's building strength, some weeks it's moving with more confidence, and some weeks it's just a bit of breathing space.

A monthly nutrition chat

30 minutes on how you're eating, what keeps tripping you up, and one or two realistic things to try next. No meal plans, no banned foods. Yes, the chocolate stays.

Your home practice plan

Simple stuff between sessions, built around how much time you've got and what equipment you have — even if that's just a mat and your living room floor.

A weekly WhatsApp check-in

Check in, ask anything, tweak what's next. I reply within two working days.

Playlists you'll want to steal, and flows that don't feel like homework. Dodgy knee? Grumpy lower back? We work around it, never through it.
