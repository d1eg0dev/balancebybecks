Questions

A few things you might be wondering

If your question isn't here, ask me on the free call — or email rebecca@balancebybecks.com.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?+

Absolutely. Everything's built around where you're starting from, and I'll always give you options.

Does online really work?+

Yes. Every session is live over video, just you and me. We'll set up your camera together in the first session so I can see your whole body and guide you properly.

What do I need?+

A mat, a bit of floor space and decent Wi-Fi. If anything else would help, we'll talk about it before you buy anything.

I don't live in Bali. Does the time difference work?+

Yes. Most clients join from the UK, Australia and across Asia, and we'll make sure our times line up before you sign up.

What times are available?+

Early mornings and evenings UK time are the most popular, and we'll find a regular slot that suits your week.

Which video platform do we use?+

Zoom, or Google Meet if you prefer. A phone propped up works fine; a laptop is even better.

Will you give me a meal plan?+

No, and that's on purpose. Meal plans tend to last about a week. We focus on understanding food, building habits and keeping you accountable, so it actually sticks.

I'm pregnant or postnatal. Can I join?+

Yes, with modifications. Tell me where you are on the call, and make sure you're well enough to exercise or have checked with your doctor.

I have a medical condition. Is this right for me?+

This is coaching for everyday health and habits. It isn't medical treatment, and it doesn't replace your doctor or dietitian. Mention it on our call and we'll work out the right support for you.

What happens after the three months?+

Most clients continue month to month at the same £250, with 30 days' notice whenever you want to stop.

What if I need to miss or move a session?+

Life happens. You can reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice, and if you're ill or away we can carry one session into the next month.

Can I just do movement, or just nutrition?+

Yes. Mention it on the call and I'll talk you through separate options and prices.
