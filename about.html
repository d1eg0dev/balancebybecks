Becks · Bali About 03

It didn’t start in a yoga studio. It started in London.

Fashion, startups, years of dancing and high expectations — always pushing to do more, be better. Eventually something had to change.

I got fascinated by stress, nutrition and movement that supports your body instead of criticising it. Not in a cleanse-your-chakras-every-five-minutes kind of way. In a real-life way — wine and chocolate included.

And now

Usually in the ocean, at reformer, or hunting the best smoothie bowl on the island.

My mission: the best version of you — because balance looks different for everyone.
