Meet Becks

It didn't start in a yoga studio. It started in London.

Fashion, startups, years of dancing and high expectations — always pushing to do more, be better. Eventually something had to change. I got fascinated by stress, nutrition and movement that supports your body instead of criticising it. Not in a cleanse-your-chakras-every-five-minutes kind of way. In a real-life way — wine and chocolate included.

Now I'm in Bali, usually in the ocean, at reformer, or on a very serious mission to find the best smoothie bowl on the island. My mission: the best version of you — because balance looks different for everyone.
