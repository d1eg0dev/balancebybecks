How online 1:1 works
Sound familiar?

You haven't lost your motivation — you've just been carrying a lot.

Most women who come to me say a version of the same thing: "I used to be active — I don't know how I got here." A busy season, an injury, a big life change… and slowly movement stops feeling like yours. Your body feels like a stranger, routine has slipped away, and the idea of walking into a gym or a group class feels impossible.

Nothing about that means you've failed. It means you're human, and that you've been doing a lot. Private 1:1 coaching exists precisely for this: a calm, judgement-free space to rebuild routine, trust and confidence at your own pace, with someone in your corner the whole way.

The programme

Become the version of you that feels good, and stays that way.

You've had glimpses of her. The you who wakes up with energy, moves without overthinking it, eats well without obsessing, and feels at home in her body. This programme is about making her your everyday, not just your good weeks.

What's included

4 private online sessions a month

50 minutes each, mixing yoga, Pilates and mobility depending on what you need. Some weeks that's building strength, some weeks it's moving with more confidence, and some weeks it's just a bit of breathing space.

A monthly nutrition chat

30 minutes looking at how you're eating, what keeps tripping you up, and one or two realistic things to try next. Think meal planning, eating regularly, and food that works with your lifestyle rather than against it.

Your home practice plan

Simple stuff to do between sessions, built around how much time you've got and what equipment you have (even if that's just a mat and your living room floor).

A weekly WhatsApp check-in

Once a week we check in on how things are going, you can ask me anything, and we tweak what's next. I reply within two working days.

Meet Becks

Balance by Becks didn't start in a yoga studio. It started in London.

I was working across fashion and startups, studying fashion and assisting on styling jobs, all while keeping up with the pace of London life. In industries where image matters so much, it's easy to fall into comparison: how you look, how you behave, how you're supposed to show up. Add years of dancing and high expectations, and I always felt like I needed to do more, be better and keep pushing. Eventually something had to change.

I got fascinated by stress, nutrition and movement that actually supports your body instead of constantly criticising it. Not in a cleanse-your-chakras-every-five-minutes kind of way. In a real-life way, where you can still drink wine, eat chocolate and enjoy life while feeling strong and balanced.

Now I live in Bali. When I'm not in the ocean or at a reformer class, you'll usually find me on a yoga mat somewhere. I've spent 5 years teaching classes, private clients, retreats and events.

My mission is simple: to help you feel like the best version of you. Not a copy of someone on Instagram, and not who you think you're supposed to be. You, feeling strong, steady and like yourself again. Because balance looks different for everyone, and that's exactly how it should be.

"Balance isn't about perfect routines. It's about knowing how to come back to yourself." — Becks

Questions

A few things you might be wondering

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?+

Absolutely. Everything's built around where you're starting from, and I'll always give you options.

What do I need?+

A mat, a bit of floor space and decent Wi-Fi. If anything else would help, we'll talk about it before you buy anything.

I don't live in Bali. Does the time difference work?+

Yes. I work with women in the UK, Australia and Asia, and we'll make sure our times line up before you sign up.

Will you give me a meal plan?+

No, and that's on purpose. Meal plans tend to last about a week. We focus on understanding food, building habits and keeping you accountable, so it actually sticks.

I have a medical condition. Is this right for me?+

This is coaching for everyday health and habits. It isn't medical treatment, and it doesn't replace your doctor or dietitian. Mention it on our call and we'll work out the right support for you.

Can I just do movement, or just nutrition?+

Yes. Mention it on our call and I'll talk you through separate options and prices.
